Section 73. Compensation for loss or damage caused by breach of contract.
A party who suffers from a breach of contract is entitled to receive compensation from the
party who broke it for loss that naturally arose in the usual course of things from the breach,
or that both parties knew, when they made the contract, would be likely to result from it.
Compensation is not given for remote and indirect loss or damage.

Section 74. Compensation for breach of contract where penalty stipulated for.
Where a contract names a sum to be paid on breach, or contains a penalty clause, the aggrieved
party is entitled to reasonable compensation not exceeding the amount named, whether or not
actual damage or loss is proved to have been caused by the breach.
