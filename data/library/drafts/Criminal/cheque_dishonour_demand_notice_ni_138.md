# Legal Demand Notice for Cheque Dishonour (Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881)

> Must be sent within 30 days of receiving the bank's return memo (s.138 proviso (b)). The drawer has 15 days from receipt to pay (proviso (c)); the complaint must be filed within one month after that period expires (s.142(1)(b)). Send by registered post AD / speed post and email; keep proof of dispatch and delivery.

3. That my client presented the said cheque through [● Bank, Branch] on [●], within its validity, and the same was returned unpaid with the remark "[● Funds Insufficient / Exceeds Arrangement / Account Closed / Payment Stopped by Drawer]" vide return memo dated [●], received by my client on [●].

I therefore call upon you to pay the said amount of ₹[●] (Rupees [● in words]) to my client within **15 days of receipt of this notice**, failing which my client shall be constrained to initiate criminal proceedings against you under Sections 138 and 142 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, besides civil proceedings for recovery, entirely at your risk as to costs and consequences.

## Drafting checklist

- Demand only the cheque amount (other sums, if claimed, should be separately stated so the demand is not vague).
- Notice addressed to the drawer (and, for a company, to the company and directors responsible under s.141).
- Date of receipt of the return memo establishes the 30-day window.
