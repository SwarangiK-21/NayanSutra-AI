## Grounds

A. Because the applicant has a reasonable apprehension of arrest and the accusation appears to have been made with the object of injuring and humiliating the applicant.
B. Because the nature and gravity of the accusation, the antecedents of the applicant and the possibility of the applicant fleeing from justice — the factors under Section 482(1) BNSS — all favour the grant of protection.
C. Because the offence(s) alleged are punishable with imprisonment up to [●] years and the evidence is documentary.
D. Because the applicant undertakes to join investigation as and when required, not to influence witnesses, and not to leave India without permission (conditions under Section 482(2) BNSS).
E. Because personal liberty under Article 21 cannot be curtailed by arrest that is not necessary.
