## Grounds

A. Because the applicant is innocent and has been falsely implicated; the allegations, even if taken at face value, do not disclose the ingredients of the offence(s) under Section(s) [●].
B. Because the dispute is essentially [civil / commercial / matrimonial] in nature and has been given a criminal colour.
C. Because the evidence is primarily documentary and already in the custody of the investigating agency; there is no possibility of tampering.
D. Because the applicant has cooperated with the investigation and is not a flight risk; the applicant is ready to surrender passport [●] and abide by any condition.
E. Because the maximum sentence for the offence(s) is [●] and the applicant has already undergone [●] of custody; prolonged pre-trial detention offends Article 21 of the Constitution. [If applicable: the applicant is entitled to consideration under Section 479 BNSS (undertrial detention for half/one-third of the maximum term).]
F. Because "bail is the rule and jail the exception", and the trial is likely to take considerable time.
G. Because the co-accused [●] has been granted bail by order dated [●] (parity) [omit if not applicable].

(a) release the applicant on regular bail in FIR No. [●] registered at Police Station [●], on such terms and conditions as this Hon'ble Court deems fit; and
(b) pass any other order deemed just in the facts and circumstances of the case.
