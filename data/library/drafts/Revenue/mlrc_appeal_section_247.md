# Appeal against Order of Revenue Officer (Sections 247–248, Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966)

> Appeals lie to the superior revenue officer named in s.247 (e.g., from Tahsildar to SDO, SDO to Collector). Limitation under s.248: first appeal within 60 days, second appeal within 90 days of the order (verify in the bare act; time to obtain a certified copy is excluded). Revision lies under s.257; review under s.258.
