# Revision Application (Section 257, Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966)

> Section 257 empowers the State Government and specified revenue/survey officers to call for and examine the record of a subordinate officer's proceedings to satisfy themselves as to the legality or propriety of a decision or order, and the regularity of the proceedings. Revision is discretionary; plead illegality, impropriety or irregularity specifically.
