# Affidavit in Support of Application / Petition

> Affidavits must be confined to facts the deponent can prove of his own knowledge, except on interlocutory applications where statements of belief may be admitted if grounds are stated (Order XIX Rule 3 CPC). Swear before an Oath Commissioner / Notary.
