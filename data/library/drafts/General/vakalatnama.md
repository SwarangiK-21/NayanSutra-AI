# Vakalatnama (Authority to Appear and Act)

> Format varies by court; many courts require their printed form with a Bar Council welfare stamp. Execute before the advocate accepts the brief.

## Form

IN THE [● COURT NAME], [●]
[● CASE TYPE] NO. [●] OF [●]

[● Party name] — Petitioner / Plaintiff / Appellant / Complainant
VERSUS
[● Party name] — Respondent / Defendant / Accused

**VAKALATNAMA**

I / We, [● name(s) of client(s)], the [● Petitioner / Respondent] in the above matter, do hereby appoint and retain [● Advocate name(s)], Advocate(s), Enrolment No(s). [●], to appear, act and plead for me / us in the above matter and in all proceedings arising therefrom, including applications, appeals, reviews and execution, and for that purpose to:

1. sign, file, verify and present pleadings, applications, petitions, affidavits and documents;
2. withdraw or compromise the matter, or refer it to arbitration or mediation, with my / our express instructions;
3. deposit and withdraw money and documents, and receive copies;
4. engage other advocates on my / our behalf, as needed.

I / We agree to ratify all acts done by the said Advocate(s) in pursuance of this authority. I / We shall pay the agreed fees and shall not hold the Advocate(s) responsible if the matter is decided ex parte due to my / our failure to give instructions or attend when required.

Signed at [●] on [●].

CLIENT(S): ____________________          ACCEPTED: ____________________
                                          [● Advocate], Advocate for [●]
