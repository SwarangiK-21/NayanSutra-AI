# Application for Adjournment (Order XVII Rule 1 CPC)

> Adjournments are granted only for sufficient cause, ordinarily not more than three times to a party during the hearing of a suit (Order XVII Rule 1 proviso), and may be on costs. Annex supporting documents (medical certificate, cause list of another court, etc.).
