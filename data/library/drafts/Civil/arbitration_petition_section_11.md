# Petition for Appointment of Arbitrator (Section 11, Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996)

> Filed before the High Court (domestic arbitration) or the Supreme Court (international commercial arbitration) after the other side fails to appoint within 30 days of a request (s.11(4)–(6)). Attach the notice invoking arbitration under s.21. At this stage the court examines primarily the existence of an arbitration agreement.
