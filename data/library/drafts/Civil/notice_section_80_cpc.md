# Notice to Government under Section 80 CPC

> No suit shall be instituted against the Government or a public officer (for acts in official capacity) until two months after notice in writing is delivered (s.80(1)). Urgent relief may be sought with leave of the court without notice (s.80(2)). The notice must state the cause of action, the name, description and residence of the plaintiff, and the relief claimed.
