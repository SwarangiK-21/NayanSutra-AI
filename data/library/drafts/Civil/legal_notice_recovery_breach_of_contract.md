# Legal Notice for Recovery of Money / Breach of Contract

> Pre-litigation notice. Limitation for compensation for breach of contract is generally 3 years from the date of breach (Limitation Act, Sch. Art. 55); for money lent, 3 years from the loan (Art. 19). For suits against the Government or a public officer, a separate 2-month notice under Section 80 CPC is mandatory.

## Notice

**LEGAL NOTICE**
By Registered Post A.D. / Speed Post / Email

Date: [●]

To,
[● Name of noticee], [● Designation, if company]
[● Address]

Sub: Breach of [● Agreement / Work Order / Purchase Order] dated [●] and demand for payment of ₹[●]

Sir/Madam,

Under instructions from my client [● Name], [● address] ("my client"), I serve you with this notice:

1. That my client and you entered into [● agreement] dated [●] whereby [● key obligations: supply of goods / services / construction / loan] for a consideration of ₹[●] (copy enclosed).
2. That my client duly performed its obligations by [● details, dates, invoices/running bills].
3. That in breach of [Clause [●] of] the agreement you [● failed to pay / terminated without cause / failed to deliver] despite reminders dated [●].
4. That as on date a sum of ₹[●] is due and payable, together with interest at [●]% per annum from [●] [as agreed / as per Section 34 CPC], and my client has suffered loss and damage which naturally arose in the usual course of things from the breach (Section 73, Indian Contract Act, 1872). [If the contract names a sum payable on breach, claim reasonable compensation not exceeding that sum — Section 74.]

I therefore call upon you to pay my client ₹[●] with interest at [●]% p.a. from [●] till payment within **15 days** of receipt of this notice, failing which my client will institute appropriate civil proceedings [and/or invoke arbitration under Clause [●]] for recovery with costs, entirely at your risk.

[● Advocate name], Advocate
Enrolment No. [●] · [Address · Phone · Email]

## Drafting checklist

- Attach copies of agreement, invoices, ledger statement, correspondence.
- Check for an arbitration clause; if present, the notice may also serve as a notice invoking arbitration (Section 21, Arbitration and Conciliation Act).
- Check whether pre-institution mediation under Section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act applies (commercial disputes of specified value).
