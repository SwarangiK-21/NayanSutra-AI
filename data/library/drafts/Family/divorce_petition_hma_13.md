# Petition for Divorce (Section 13(1) Hindu Marriage Act, 1955)

> Grounds under s.13(1) include cruelty (ia), desertion for 2 years (ib), conversion (ii), unsoundness of mind (iii), etc. File before the Family Court having jurisdiction under s.19 HMA (where the marriage was solemnised, the respondent resides, the parties last resided together, or the wife resides). No petition within one year of marriage without leave (s.14).
