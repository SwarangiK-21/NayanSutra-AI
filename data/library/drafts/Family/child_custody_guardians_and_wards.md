# Petition for Custody / Guardianship of a Minor (Sections 7, 10 and 25, Guardians and Wards Act, 1890)

> The paramount consideration is the welfare of the minor (s.17). Jurisdiction lies with the court where the minor ordinarily resides (s.9). For Hindus, read with the Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, 1956. Section 25 governs return of a ward removed from the guardian's custody.
