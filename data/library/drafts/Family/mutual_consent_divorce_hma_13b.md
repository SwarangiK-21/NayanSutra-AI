# Joint Petition for Divorce by Mutual Consent (Section 13B Hindu Marriage Act, 1955)

> Requirements: living separately for one year or more, inability to live together, and mutual agreement that the marriage be dissolved (s.13B(1)). The second motion is made not earlier than 6 months and not later than 18 months after the first (s.13B(2)); courts may waive the 6-month period in appropriate cases — cite the governing judgment only if it is in your sources.
