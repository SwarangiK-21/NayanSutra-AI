NyaySutra AI

An agentic retrieval-augmented research assistant for advocates. Answers are grounded in your case files and official bare acts, with numbered citations and an automatic citation audit.

Data sources

  • Bare Acts: India Code — Legislative Department, Ministry of Law & Justice (indiacode.gov.in)
  • IPC text: eCourts district court portal (Govt. of India)
  • Judgments: Indian Kanoon
  • Legal news headlines: LiveLaw RSS feed (links open on livelaw.in)
  • Case files: uploaded by you, visible only to the people on the case

Disclaimer

AI-generated research aid, not legal advice. Verify every citation against the primary source before relying on it.
