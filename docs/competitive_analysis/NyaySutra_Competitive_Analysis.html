10. Defensible position

Maturity assessment vs market ranking
QuestionAnswer
What can be rankedPrototype maturity against a capability rubric — yes. Market rank among commercial products — no: NyaySutra has no users, pricing or deployment, and most competitors' AI could not be tested.
Prototype maturity assessment4.95 / 10 on the weighted rubric (100% of criteria evidenced, confidence Medium). Strongest: guardrails, routing, workflow breadth. Weakest: corpus, case law, maturity.
Provisional tier (not a rank)Tier 3 of 4 — 'Advanced prototype / pre-commercial'. Tier 1 = national incumbents (SCC Online, Manupatra, Indian Kanoon, LexisNexis); Tier 2 = funded AI-native products with users (CaseMine, Bharat.Law, NyayAssist, Lexlegis); Tier 3 = working prototypes with real architecture; Tier 4 = demos.
Why no India-wide or global rankCompetitor AI accuracy is unmeasured in public (except a 2024 US study of Lexis/Westlaw, S20); we could not sign in to any competitor; vendor claims dominate. A rank would be invented.
Most comparable todayBharat.Law (price, languages), Indian Kanoon PRISM (free tier), NyayAssist (research + workflow for small chambers).
