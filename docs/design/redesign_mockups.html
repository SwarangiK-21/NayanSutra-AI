What must be proved in a cheque bounce complaint under Section 138?
Searched 5 ways · 8 passages used · citations checked

To succeed under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, the complainant generally needs to show these points 1:

  1. The cheque was drawn on the accused's account to discharge a legally enforceable debt or liability.
  2. It was presented within six months or its validity period, whichever is earlier.
  3. The bank returned it unpaid for insufficient funds or a similar reason.
  4. A written demand notice was sent within 30 days of learning of the return.
  5. The drawer failed to pay within 15 days of receiving the notice.

The complaint must then be filed within one month of the cause of action 2.

Dishonour of cheque for insufficiency, etc., of funds in the account

Where any cheque drawn by a person on an account maintained by him with a banker for payment of any amount of money to another person… is returned by the bank unpaid… such person shall be deemed to have committed an offence and shall… be punished with imprisonment for a term which may be extended to two years, or with fine which may extend to twice the amount of the cheque, or with both.

When the section applies

6 months

Present the cheque within this time or its validity, whichever is earlier

30 days

Send a written demand after learning the cheque was returned

15 days

The drawer may pay after receiving the notice
